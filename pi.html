Biography

Prof. Haw-Tyng Huang/ 黃晧庭

Professor Haw-Tyng Huang established and leads the first laboratory at National Taiwan University dedicated to electronic ceramics.

He received his Ph.D. in Materials Science and Engineering from Pennsylvania State University in 2019. His doctoral dissertation focused on the synthesis and characterization of carbon nanomaterials.

After completing his Ph.D., he worked at Stony Brook University and Brookhaven National Laboratory as a postdoctoral researcher with the Joint Photon Sciences Institute, working with Professor John Parise and Professor Lars Ehm.

He later joined Apple as a display scientist, applying his materials science background to next-generation display technology. His subsequent work at the Industrial Technology Research Institute focused on specialty semiconductor development and project leadership.
