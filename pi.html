Biography

Advancing functional materials through connected science.

Professor [Name] leads the Electronic Ceramics Laboratory at National Taiwan University. Their research examines how crystal structure, defects, interfaces, and processing histories govern the functional response of dielectric, ferroelectric, and piezoelectric materials.

The group combines ceramic processing and thin-film deposition with electrical, structural, and thermal characterization. This integrated approach supports both fundamental discovery and the translation of promising materials into reliable components.

Professor [Name] is committed to rigorous graduate training, interdisciplinary collaboration, and creating an environment in which researchers learn to connect careful experimental design with clear scientific communication.

Research interests

  • Ferroelectric ceramics
  • Dielectric energy storage
  • Functional thin films
  • Interface engineering
  • Impedance spectroscopy
  • Materials reliability
