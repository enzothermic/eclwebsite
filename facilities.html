Equipment & facilities

A connected path from powder to performance.

Our processing and characterization platform supports reproducible studies across bulk ceramics, thin films, printed structures, and integrated electro-ceramic components.

Core capabilities

Equipment for the complete workflow.

Instrument access and operating conditions are coordinated through the laboratory. Contact us to discuss collaboration.

Thermal processing

High-Temperature Tube & Muffle Furnaces

Controlled-atmosphere sintering, calcination, annealing, and thermal-profile development for ceramic and film systems.

Sintering · Annealing · Calcination

Thin-film deposition

RF/DC Magnetron Sputtering System

Deposition of functional oxides, electrodes, and multilayers with controlled gas chemistry and substrate conditions.

Oxides · Electrodes · Multilayers

Powder processing

Precision Planetary Ball Mill

High-energy mixing, particle-size refinement, and mechanochemical synthesis with tunable media and processing time.

Mixing · Milling · Synthesis

Electrical characterization

High-Precision Impedance & LCR Analyzer

Frequency- and temperature-dependent dielectric, impedance, and equivalent-circuit analysis.

Impedance · Dielectrics · LCR

Phase analysis

X-Ray Diffraction Microstructural Mapping

Phase identification, lattice refinement, texture evaluation, and spatially resolved structural mapping.

Phase · Texture · Lattice

Thick-film fabrication

Screen Printing & Tape Casting Systems

Patterned electrode printing and uniform green-sheet fabrication for multilayer structures and test coupons.

Printing · Casting · Patterning

Experimental workflow

Designed for iteration, not isolated measurements.

  1. STEP 01

    Prepare

    Powder synthesis, mixing, and forming.

  2. STEP 02

    Process

    Thermal treatment and deposition.

  3. STEP 03

    Characterize

    Structural and electrical evaluation.

  4. STEP 04

    Refine

    Correlate results and iterate.
