Teaching approach

From physical intuition to defensible conclusions.

Students learn to connect equations and models with the real behavior of imperfect materials and measurement systems.

01

Build the physical picture

Start from crystal structure, defects, interfaces, and energy landscapes.

02

Design the measurement

Choose experiments that can distinguish between competing explanations.

03

Interrogate the data

Treat uncertainty, assumptions, and alternative interpretations explicitly.

04

Communicate the evidence

Present concise arguments supported by transparent analysis and visuals.

Research mentoring

Graduate development in the laboratory.

Laboratory mentoring combines structured technical training with increasing research independence. Students develop experimental plans, maintain traceable records, analyze data critically, present work regularly, and contribute to collaborative problem solving.

Student outcomes

  • 4Communicate results through papers, presentations, and technical discussion.
