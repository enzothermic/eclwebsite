Research

Functional ceramics from atomic order to device response.

We investigate how composition, processing, interfaces, and microstructure govern the electrical and electromechanical behavior of advanced ceramic systems.

Core research pillars

An integrated materials platform.

01

Bulk functional materials

Dielectric & Piezoelectric Ceramics

We design and process ferroelectric, relaxor, and piezoelectric ceramics with controlled phase assemblage, grain structure, and defect chemistry.

High-density energy storageCrystal-structure engineeringHigh-power actuatorsFerroelectric phase control
02

Integrated material systems

Functional Thin-Film Components

We control film composition, texture, residual stress, and interfaces to integrate functional oxides with electrodes, substrates, and microelectronic platforms.

Advanced vacuum depositionReactive sputtering profilesInterface optimizationComponent integration
03

Multiscale measurement

Electro-Ceramic Characterization

We resolve electrical, structural, and thermal behavior across frequency, temperature, field, and length scale to reveal dominant mechanisms and failure modes.

Impedance spectroscopyThermal microstructure mappingPhase-transition analysisReliability under field

Cross-cutting questions

Questions that connect our projects.

How do interfaces control polarization?

Linking local chemistry and stress with macroscopic dielectric response.

What limits high-field reliability?

Identifying transport pathways, defects, and thermal concentration.

Can processing encode performance?

Using thermal and deposition histories to direct structure formation.

How does scale change behavior?

Comparing bulk, film, multilayer, and component-level responses.
